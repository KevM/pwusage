Payback mockups (Savings Phase 3)

A new Payback section on the Solar System screen, directly under Savings, and the System Costs sheet it opens. Figures are examples. Every amount says “about”; every projection lists its assumptions.

What to check

  1. Placement Payback is its own section under Savings on Solar System. iOS only; no web screen.
  2. Seasons “around spring 2031” uses Mar–May spring, Jun–Aug summer, Sep–Nov fall, Dec–Feb winter (December counts toward the next year’s winter).
  3. Credit The rate pre-fills from the install year (30% through 2019, 26% for 2020–2021, 30% for 2022–2025, off from 2026, when the federal home credit ended) and stays editable.
  4. Powerwall date Pre-fills from the first day the Powerwall ran. Once saved, it also sets when the Powerwall enters the savings scenarios.
  5. Caveats Coverage, estimated-share and Fix buttons reuse the Phase 2 lines. Break-even waits for 300 counted days in the last 12 months.
